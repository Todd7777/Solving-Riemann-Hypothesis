# Bibliography

Primary sources for the statements in these notes. (Items marked † are quoted from memory of the literature and should be checked against the originals before being cited elsewhere.)

## Foundational
- B. Riemann, *Über die Anzahl der Primzahlen unter einer gegebenen Grösse*, Monatsber. Berlin Akad. (1859).
- H. M. Edwards, *Riemann's Zeta Function*, Academic Press 1974 (Dover 2001). The Riemann–Siegel formula, Turing's method, Gram's law.
- E. C. Titchmarsh, *The Theory of the Riemann Zeta-Function*, 2nd ed. revised by D. R. Heath-Brown, OUP 1986.
- H. Iwaniec, E. Kowalski, *Analytic Number Theory*, AMS 2004 (Thm 5.12: explicit formula).
- E. Bombieri, *Problems of the Millennium: The Riemann Hypothesis*, Clay Mathematics Institute (2000).
- J. B. Conrey, *The Riemann Hypothesis*, Notices AMS 50 (2003) 341–353.
- P. Borwein, S. Choi, B. Rooney, A. Weirathmueller (eds.), *The Riemann Hypothesis: A Resource for the Afficionado and Virtuoso Alike*, Springer 2008 (reprints of Nyman–Beurling, Li, Lagarias, Bombieri, Connes, …).
- K. Broughan, *Equivalents of the Riemann Hypothesis*, vols. 1–2 (CUP 2017), vol. 3 (2023).
